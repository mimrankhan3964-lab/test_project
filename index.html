💧 Water Cycle the complete continuous journey of water

Watch the full cycle: Ocean → Evaporation → Clouds → Rain → Mountains → River → Groundwater → back to Ocean.

📖 Definition

The Water Cycle is the continuous movement of water between the ocean, atmosphere, land, and underground. The Sun's heat drives it, and gravity pulls water back down.

Teacher's Note: Ask: "Where does rain come from?" (Clouds.) "Where do clouds come from?" (Evaporated water.) The same water has cycled for millions of years.

💡 Explanation

The Sun heats water in oceans and lakes → evaporation. Plants release vapour → transpiration. Vapour rises, cools, and forms clouds → condensation. Water falls as rain → precipitation. It flows down mountains into rivers (runoff), seeps into the ground (infiltration), and returns to the ocean — and the cycle repeats.

  • Evaporation: Liquid water → water vapour.
  • Transpiration: Plants release water vapour.
  • Condensation: Water vapour → clouds.
  • Precipitation: Rain, snow, sleet, hail.
  • Runoff: Water flows over land into rivers.
  • Infiltration: Water seeps into the ground.
  • Collection: Water gathers in oceans and lakes.

🌊 The Seven Stages

1. Evaporation

Sun heats water → vapour rises from oceans.

2. Transpiration

Plants release water vapour through leaves.

3. Condensation

Vapour cools → forms clouds.

4. Precipitation

Rain, snow, or hail falls.

5. Runoff

Water flows over land into rivers.

6. Infiltration

Water seeps into soil and rocks.

7. Collection

Water gathers in oceans and lakes.

🌍 Day & Night Earth's rotation creates day and night

The Earth spins on its axis once every 24 hours. The side facing the Sun has day, the other side has night.

📖 Definition

Day and Night are caused by the Earth's rotation on its axis. As the Earth spins, the side facing the Sun experiences day, and the opposite side experiences night.

Teacher's Note: Shine a torch on a globe. The lit side = day, the dark side = night. Rotate the globe slowly — day and night change places.

💡 Explanation

The Earth is a sphere that rotates once every 24 hours. The half facing the Sun is lit (day) and the other half is dark (night). As the Earth turns eastwards, the Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west.

  • Axis: Imaginary line from North to South Pole.
  • Rotation: One complete turn = 24 hours = one day.
  • Day side: Half facing the Sun.
  • Night side: Half facing away.
  • Sunrise & Sunset: Caused by eastward rotation.

🌞 Key Facts

Rotation Time

24 hours for one full turn.

Axis Tilt

23.5° tilt — creates seasons too.

Direction

West to East — Sun rises in the east.

Day Side

Half facing the Sun = daytime.

Night Side

Half facing away = night.

Result

Every place gets both day and night daily.
